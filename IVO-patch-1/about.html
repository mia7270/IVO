About IVO One

IVO One is a digital payment platform designed to make the use of digital assets for everyday spending more practical, secure and accessible.

Our platform connects supported digital assets with real-world payment capabilities, enabling eligible users to fund their account and use the IVO One Debit Visa Card for everyday purchases through supported Visa payment channels.

By combining digital asset infrastructure with established payment networks, IVO One aims to provide a seamless and familiar payment experience while maintaining a strong focus on security, compliance and operational reliability.

We believe digital assets should be easier to use in everyday life. Our goal is to simplify the payment experience and provide users with a practical way to move from digital assets to real-world spending.

Our Vision

To create a more accessible payment experience where digital assets can be used easily and efficiently for everyday transactions.

Our Mission

To connect digital assets with real-world payments through secure, reliable and user-friendly payment solutions.

What We Stand For

Practical Payments

We focus on turning digital assets into practical payment capabilities for everyday use.

Simple Experience

Security & Compliance

Security and responsible operations are fundamental to our platform. We work with established payment, technology and compliance partners to support appropriate customer verification, transaction monitoring and payment processing.

Reliable Infrastructure

IVO One brings together digital asset and payment infrastructure to provide a dependable experience from funding through to card spending.

Built for Everyday Use

Our focus is simple: enabling customers to use supported digital assets through a familiar card payment experience.
