ASK A QUESTION
What is IVO ONE? +

A prepaid card you top up with USDT or USDC. Once loaded, it spends like any other card — in store, online, and at ATMs, wherever it's accepted.

How do I top it up? +

Send USDT or USDC to your card's deposit address from any wallet or exchange. Your spendable balance updates in seconds — no bank transfer, no waiting days.

Where can I use it? +

Accepted by 40M+ merchants in 200+ countries. Pay in 130+ currencies. Withdraw at 1.2M+ ATMs worldwide.

What do I need to sign up? +

Just your ID and a selfie. Our KYC takes minutes, not weeks — and your virtual card is ready the moment you're approved.

Is my money safe? +

Freeze the card in one tap, 3DS authentication on every online purchase, instant alerts for every transaction, and PIN changes right from the app.
